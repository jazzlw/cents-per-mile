How the math works. Gas: dollars per gallon ÷ MPG. Electric: dollars per kWh ÷ miles per kWh. Nothing else — this compares fuel only, not insurance, tires, maintenance, registration, or depreciation.

Where the ratings come from. EPA combined city/highway figures pulled from fueleconomy.gov; every vehicle keeps its EPA database ID so any number can be re-checked at the source. Model years span 2017–2027: current ratings where the EPA lists them, older years for vehicles no longer sold, and 2027 for one that is only just rated. EV efficiency is measured from the wall outlet, so the roughly 10–15% lost to charging is already subtracted. Every gas vehicle here is rated on regular unleaded, so one gas price applies to all of them.

One caveat on charging. The price gap between charging at home overnight and pulling into a DC fast charger is bigger than the gap between any two cars on this chart. Try both presets.
